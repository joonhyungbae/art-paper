# Art-Research Evidence Model

Replaces the empirical **evidence hierarchy** (meta-analysis > RCT > cohort > case report > expert opinion) used by ARS. Consumed across art-paper wherever a claim must be "supported."

> **Why a different model:** In practice-based art research the knowledge produced is *situated* — it arises from making a specific work in a specific context. It is not generalizable in the statistical sense, and ranking it against RCTs is a category error. The art paper's job is to make a **defensible, well-evidenced argument about an artwork and what it reveals**, not to establish a population-level finding.

---

## 1. The artwork is primary evidence

The single most important inversion from ARS: **the artwork itself is the primary source of evidence**, not the literature. A claim about what a work does, means, or achieves is grounded first in the work, then corroborated by documentation, context, and reception.

## 2. Evidence stack (not a hierarchy — a triangulation)

These are complementary lenses, not a ranking. A strong art paper triangulates across several.

| Evidence type | What it grounds | Documentation expected |
|---|---|---|
| **The work as encountered** | claims about form, experience, materiality | description, stills, video, diagrams, live demo |
| **Process & making** | claims about intent, iteration, technical realization | process notes, code/system description, fabrication record, version history |
| **Exhibition & reception** | claims about how the work functions with an audience | venue/date, install photos, observed/recorded responses, press, curatorial framing |
| **Conceptual lineage** | claims about positioning and contribution | precedent artworks, artist statements, theory, criticism (cited) |
| **Situated reflection** | claims about insight gained through practice | the author's reasoned account, made falsifiable by the above |

## 3. What "supported" means in art-paper

A claim is supported when it is anchored to at least one evidence type above **and** that anchor is documented enough that a reader (or reviewer) could in principle verify it. Concretely:

- A claim about the **work** → anchored to a figure/media reference or a concrete description.
- A claim about **reception** → anchored to a named venue/date + observable detail, never "audiences loved it."
- A claim about **precedent / discourse** → anchored to a real citation (ACM Reference Format). The L3 citation-faithfulness gate applies unchanged.
- A claim about **technical realization** → anchored to a description specific enough to be plausible (system, method, tools).

## 4. Claims that require extra scrutiny (integrity gate)

The Stage 2.5 / 4.5 integrity gate re-scopes ARS's "statistical data verification" to **artwork & realization claim verification**. Flag for verification:

1. **Reception inflation** — "widely acclaimed," "audiences were moved," with no observable anchor. → Down-scope to what was actually observed/recorded.
2. **Precedence/novelty claims** — "the first work to…" → require citation evidence or hedge.
3. **Technical claims** — "real-time," "novel algorithm," "fully autonomous" → require a realization anchor; watch for fabricated capability.
4. **Attribution/credit** — collaborative work described as solo, or contributors unnamed. → cross-check against §collaboration credit.
5. **Exhibition claims** — venues/dates/awards. → must be real and citable; treated like citation faithfulness.

These flags **block** the gate exactly as citation/data issues do in ARS (same blocking semantics, same max-3-round fix loop).

## 5. Honest hedging is a virtue, not a weakness

Practice-based insight is legitimately partial and situated. art-paper preserves the ARS protected-hedging contract: upstream-marked hedges ("in this installation," "for these visitors," "we observed, though did not measure") must survive downstream editing. Over-claiming generalizability is a worse failure than honest specificity — reviewers in this genre punish the former and reward the latter.

## 6. What art-paper does NOT import from ARS

- No evidence *ranking* (no "meta-analysis beats case report").
- No PRISMA systematic-review apparatus as a quality bar (it remains available only for Pattern 5 / lit-review mode).
- No statistical-power or reliability/validity framing as a default requirement.
- No assumption that more citations = more rigor. In art papers, a few precisely positioned references beat a dense citation wall.
