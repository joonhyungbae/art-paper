# Art-and-Technology Venues — Context Reference for the Jury

This document is used by `field_analyst_agent` and `eic_agent` (Jury Chair) as a reference for calibrating the Chair's identity and assessing **venue / program fit** for a practice-based art-research paper.

> **Do NOT treat this as a fixed authority.** Art-and-technology venues — their tracks, length limits, anonymization rules, and selectivity — drift year to year. This reference lists venue *categories* and their general character only. **Always verify the specific expectations against the current Call for Art Papers** before asserting them in a review. Do not invent impact factors, acceptance rates, or named-issue facts.

---

## 1. Primary target for art-paper

| Venue / Program | Character (verify against current CFP) |
|---|---|
| **SIGGRAPH Asia Art Papers track** | Practice-based art-and-technology papers; proceedings published on the ACM Digital Library (verify category/venue against the current CFP). The default target this skill is configured for. |
| **SIGGRAPH (North America) Art Papers / Art Gallery** | Sibling program; similar practice-based emphasis. |
| **_Leonardo_ / _Leonardo Music Journal_ (MIT Press)** | Peer-reviewed journal of art, science, and technology; a standalone art-and-technology venue and a journal an art paper may cite. (It is NOT the publication venue for the SIGGRAPH Asia Art Papers track, whose proceedings are on the ACM Digital Library.) |

## 2. Adjacent art-and-technology venues (by category)

| Category | Examples of the kind of venue | Typical emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Festival / exhibition with proceedings | Ars Electronica, ISEA (International Symposium on Electronic Art) | Exhibition record + accompanying text; conceptual and exhibition framing |
| HCI-adjacent art/design tracks | CHI alt.chi / Interactivity, DIS, TEI, NIME (for sound/instrument work) | Research-through-design / practice; some empirical bridge (Pattern 5) |
| Media-art / digital-art journals | journals of digital/media/computational art | Conceptual depth, criticism, documentation |
| Generative / computational art venues | generative-art and creative-coding programs | Realization + generative-systems lineage |
| Bio-art / art-science venues | art-science programs and journals | Living-media practice, ethics, hybrid contribution |

## 3. How the Jury Chair uses this reference

1. **Match medium to venue category.** Use the field_analyst Card's medium and ambition dimension to place the work, not a fixed ranking.
2. **State fit in terms of character, not numbers.** "This work's conceptual ambition fits a SIGGRAPH Asia Art Papers-level program" — not an invented acceptance rate.
3. **Always append the CFP caveat** to any venue-specific expectation (length, anonymization, documentation requirements).
4. **Suggest alternatives by category** when a work is out of scope (e.g., "this may sit better in a festival exhibition track than in a peer-reviewed art paper").

## 4. What this reference deliberately omits

- No impact factors, no acceptance-rate figures, no "tier" numbers — these are empirical-journal conventions that do not transfer to art venues and would be fabricated here.
- No fixed acceptance criteria for SIGGRAPH Asia Art Papers — those are set by the current CFP, which the Chair must verify.
