# Pipeline Usage Example

```
User: I want to write a paper about AI in higher education quality assurance, from research to submission

Step 1: art-inquiry -> Research report
Step 2: art-paper -> Paper first draft
Step 3: integrity check -> 100% verification of references/data
Step 4: art-reviewer (full) -> 5 review reports + Revision Roadmap
Step 5: art-paper (revision) -> Revised manuscript
Step 6: art-reviewer (re-review) -> Verification review
Step 7: (if needed) art-paper (revision) -> Second revised manuscript
Step 8: integrity check (final) -> Final 100% verification
Step 9: art-paper (format-convert) -> Final paper
```
