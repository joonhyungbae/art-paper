Trigger the `art-paper` skill in `work-doc` mode. Lays out the documentation that serves as the art paper's primary evidence: work stills, install/exhibition photos, system/process diagrams, and media frames — each with proper image-credit / courtesy lines and acmart `\includegraphics` integration. Records venue+date for exhibition entries (the L3 locator for artwork citations). Fidelity spectrum, medium oversight.
