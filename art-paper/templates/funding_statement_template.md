# Funding / Support Acknowledgment Template (Art Papers)

## Usage

Use this template to record all support that made the **artwork and its exhibition** possible — grants, residencies, commissions, production/fabrication support, in-kind technical support, and institutional support — and to generate the Acknowledgments wording for an art paper.

Reference: `references/funding_statement_guide.md`

> Keep **support** (this template) distinct from **collaboration credit** (named contributors who conceived/realized the work — see `credit_statement_template.md`) and from **image courtesy / copyright lines**. A funder or in-kind sponsor is acknowledged here, not credited as a co-author. Verify required placement and wording against the current SIGGRAPH Asia Art Papers CFP / ACM policy.

---

## Step 2: Select Statement Type

Choose the appropriate skeleton and fill in the details. Match the statement language to the paper language.

### A. Grant-supported (single source)

```
Acknowledgments: This work was supported by [Funder Full Name]
([Programme / Grant Type], Grant No. [Number]).
```

### B. Grant-supported (multiple sources)

```
Acknowledgments: This work was supported by [Funder 1] (Grant No. [Number 1]);
[Funder 2] (Grant No. [Number 2]); and [Funder 3] (Grant No. [Number 3]).
```

### C. Residency

```
Acknowledgments: This work was developed during a residency at
[Host Institution / Lab], [City], [Month Year – Month Year].
```

### D. Commission

```
Acknowledgments: [Work Title] was commissioned by [Commissioning Body]
for [Occasion / Festival / Exhibition], [Year].
```

### E. Production / in-kind support

```
Acknowledgments: Fabrication / technical support was provided by [Provider].
[Hardware / software / materials] were supported by [Sponsor].
```

### F. No external support

```
Acknowledgments: This work received no external funding or production support.
```

---

## Step 3: Funder-Mandated Disclaimer (if required)

Some public arts-council / research-council funders require a disclaimer quoted **verbatim**. Reproduce it exactly:

```
The views expressed are those of the author(s) and do not necessarily reflect
those of [Funder].
```

---

## Step 4: Combined Acknowledgments Block (example)

A complete art-paper Acknowledgments block weaves support together with collaboration credit and courtesy lines (each kept distinct):

```markdown
## Acknowledgments

[Work Title] was commissioned by [Festival] (2025) and developed during a
residency at [Media-Art Lab], [City] (2024–2025), with support from
[Arts Council] (Grant No. [Number]). Fabrication was supported by [Provider].

[Collaboration credit — see credit_statement_template.md: e.g., "Sound by
[Name]; custom electronics by [Name]."]

[Image courtesy / photo credit lines as required: e.g., "Installation views:
Photo [Name], courtesy of the artist and [Venue]."]
```
