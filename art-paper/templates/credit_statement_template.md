# Collaboration & Contribution Credit Template (Art Papers)

## Usage

Use this template to record **who did what on the work** and to generate the credit statement for an art paper. Two forms are provided:

- **Form A — Free-form maker credit lines** (preferred for the work itself; matches festival/exhibition convention). Use Steps 1 + 3A.
- **Form B — CRediT grid** (for venues whose submission system collects CRediT roles, and for Pattern 5 hybrids). Use Steps 1 + 2 + 3B.

Reference: `references/credit_authorship_guide.md`

> Keep maker credit distinct from **support/acknowledgment** (grants, residencies, in-kind — `funding_statement_template.md`) and from **image courtesy / copyright lines**. Presenting collaborative work as solo, or omitting a named maker, is an integrity flag.

## Step 3A: Output — Free-Form Maker Credit (Form A)

Name every contributor whose work shaped the piece, by what they did:

```
Concept and direction: [Name]
Generative software: [Name]
Custom electronics / firmware: [Name]
Sound: [Name]
Fabrication: [Name]
Performers: [Names]
```

### Example (Form A)

```
Concept and direction: A. Rivera
Real-time generative system: A. Rivera, K. Tan
Custom sensor electronics: K. Tan
Sound design: M. Okada
Fabrication and installation: studio team (see Acknowledgments)
```

*If the work is genuinely solo, state "Concept, software, and fabrication: [Name]" rather than leaving credit implicit.*

## Step 3B: Output — CRediT Statement (Form B)

Based on the matrix above, generate the statement in this format (match the language to the paper):

```
Author Contributions:
[Contributor 1]: [Role 1], [Role 2], [Role 3].
[Contributor 2]: [Role 1], [Role 2].
[Contributor 3]: [Role 1], [Role 2], [Role 3].
```

### Example (art collaboration, English)

```
Author Contributions:
A. Rivera: Conceptualization, Methodology (system of making), Funding acquisition,
Writing – original draft, Supervision, Project administration.
K. Tan: Software, Investigation (making/prototyping), Resources (custom electronics),
Visualization (documentation), Writing – review & editing.
M. Okada: Investigation (sound), Validation, Writing – review & editing.
```

### Example (art collaboration, second language)

```
Author Contributions:
A. Rivera: Conceptualization, Methodology (system of making), Funding acquisition,
Writing -- original draft, Supervision, Project administration.
K. Tan: Software, Investigation (making/prototyping), Resources (custom electronics),
Visualization (documentation), Writing -- review & editing.
M. Okada: Investigation (sound), Validation, Writing -- review & editing.
```

## Quality Checklist

| Check | Status |
|-------|:------:|
| Every named maker/collaborator who shaped the work is credited (Form A or B) | ☐ |
| Each role used has at least one contributor; non-applicable roles omitted | ☐ |
| At least one contributor marked as "Lead" for Writing -- original draft (Form B) | ☐ |
| At least one contributor marked for Writing -- review & editing (Form B) | ☐ |
| Corresponding author identified | ☐ |
| Maker credit kept distinct from support/acknowledgment and image courtesy lines | ☐ |
| Collaborative work not presented as solo (integrity flag) | ☐ |
| AI is NOT listed as an author; AI usage disclosed in two channels (see CFP) | ☐ |
| Equal contribution properly noted (if applicable) | ☐ |
| Credit statement language matches the paper language | ☐ |

## Notes

- Credit should be discussed and agreed upon by **all collaborators** before submission
- For most artworks, **Form A (free-form maker credits)** is the clearer, convention-matching output; use the Form B CRediT grid mainly for venue-required metadata and Pattern 5 hybrids
- The contribution matrix is an internal record; the credit statement is what appears in the paper
- Some venues collect CRediT in their submission system — use this template to prepare, then enter the data there
- For genuinely solo works, state the sole author's credit explicitly rather than leaving it implicit
- Funders, in-kind sponsors, and people who provided only space/equipment/labour are acknowledged as **support**, not credited as makers (see `funding_statement_template.md`)
