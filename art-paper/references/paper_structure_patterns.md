## Why this deferral exists

art-paper is for **practice-based art research papers** (SIGGRAPH Asia Art Papers track → ACM Digital Library). The empirical 6-pattern catalog this file used to carry (IMRaD-as-default + thematic literature review + theoretical / case-study / policy-brief / conference) does not fit the genre: in an art paper **the artwork is the argument**, and structure serves the work rather than a fixed Method/Results contract.

## The five art-paper patterns (summary — see shared reference for full structures)

| Pattern | Use when | Default? |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Practice-Based Art Paper** | One artwork (or tight series) you made; insight emerges through making | **YES (default)** |
| 2. Artist Statement / Project Description | Short, concept-forward; the `artist-statement` mode | no |
| 3. Critical / Theoretical Art Essay | A concept/critique discussing others' works as primary material | no |
| 4. Series / Portfolio Paper | A body of work over time; the contribution is the trajectory | no |
| 5. Art-Science Hybrid (IMRaD-leaning) | A genuine empirical/technical contribution alongside the artistic one | no — **only** path to IMRaD |

**Never force IMRaD onto a practice-based contribution** — that flattens the work into a "system" and is the canonical art-paper failure mode. When unsure, default to Pattern 1 and confirm with the author.
